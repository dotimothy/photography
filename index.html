Drag to rotate · Tap the camera screen to look closer

Interactive camera: V looks through the viewfinder, Enter or Space presses the shutter, left and right arrows browse photos, C opens full galleries, M or H returns to the camera home screen, L opens live space view, I inspects the photo when AI is enabled, S opens settings, Z cycles the lens from 18 to 300 millimeters, and Escape steps back. Tap the lens or top dial to zoom, scroll the mouse wheel over the lens or displays to zoom, use plus and minus for finer adjustments, drag a zoomed photo to pan, or use its overview map. Shift plus arrow keys pans; Home recenters. On mobile, pinch to zoom, double-tap to zoom in or reset, swipe an unzoomed photo to browse, and drag the live view to aim. Exit Camera View is always available at the top-left; Camera Menu is below the display.
