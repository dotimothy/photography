Journey

My passion began years ago, watching my father capture family memories on his vintage Canon T2i. What started as a hobby evolved into a relentless pursuit to paint thousands of words with the pictures I take.

Photographer

I specialize in astrophotography, capturing the dusty glow of the Milky Way and the deep colors of the Orion Nebula. By day, I traverse National Parks to honor nature through landscape photography.

Engineer

With a Master's degree in Electrical and Computer Engineering from UCLA, I now work in Computer Vision and Image Processing. Understanding image acquisition, signal processing, and visual AI allows me to push the boundaries of what a camera can capture.

Explorer

As an Eagle Scout with the photography merit badge, nature is my second home. This portfolio is my window into a collection of moments where the universe stood still just long enough for the shutter to click.
