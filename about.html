Journey

My passion began years ago, watching my father capture family memories on his vintage Canon T2i. What started as a hobby evolved into a relentless pursuit to paint thousands of words with the pictures I take. From a dark scenic space to the vast wilderness, photography became my language for showcasing the ever-changing beauty of the world.

Photographer

My lens is drawn to the vast scenes around the earth. In the darkness of the night, I specialize in astrophotography, capturing the dusty glow of the Milky Way and the deep colors of the Orion Nebula far away from artificial light pollution. By day, I traverse National and State Parks to honor the creations of Mother Nature through landscape photography.

Engineer

With a Master's degree in Electrical and Computer Engineering from UCLA, I bring technical precision to the art of capturing a picture. Understanding image acquisition, sensor mechanics, and signal processing allows me to push the boundaries of what a camera can capture, blending technology with creativity.

Explorer

From the depths of the cosmos to the raw display of national parks, I capture the most distinct parts of a specific place. As an Eagle Scout with the photography merit badge, nature is my second home. This portfolio is my window into a collection of moments where the universe stood still just long enough for the shutter to click.
